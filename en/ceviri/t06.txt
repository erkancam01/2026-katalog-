1913|INFORMATION
1914|AND MAINTENANCE
1915|Opening
1916|Turn the handle fully through 180°. The sash lifts off the gaskets onto its carriages.
1917|Sliding
1918|Slide the sash by holding the handle or grip. Move it without forcing and without striking the stopper.
1919|Closing
1920|Bring the sash to the closed position and turn the handle back to its starting position. The sash lowers into place and locks against the gaskets.
1921|WARNINGS
1922|Do not turn the handle to the closed position while the sash is open.
1923|Keep hands clear of the gap between sash and frame while the sash is moving.
1924|Do not hang extra loads on the sash; do not remove the stoppers.
1925|Do not let children use the sash unsupervised.
1926|MAINTENANCE
1927|RECOMMENDED
1928|Rail and channel:
1929|Regularly clean the bottom rail and channel of dust, sand and debris.
1930|Moving parts:
1931|Lubricate the espagnolette, locking points and carriage rollers with acid- and resin-free oil.
1932|Surfaces:
1933|Use a neutral cleaner and a soft cloth; do not use abrasive or solvent-based products.
1934|Fixings:
1935|Check that the handle and lock screws are tight.
1936|Adjustment and repair:
1937|Must only be carried out by authorised, trained personnel.
