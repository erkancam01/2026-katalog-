WHO WE ARE

Since 2003 we have been supplying innovative, technological products to the construction sector and the facade accessories market. As Birikim Kalıp Yapı Ürünleri, we manufacture for hundreds of customers in Türkiye and in many countries around the world.

WHAT WE MAKE

Facade friction stays, parallel stays, window and door handles, tilt & turn systems, hinges and lift & slide sets. We are a domestic manufacturer of the stays used on aluminium window profiles.

HOW WE WORK

With our expert engineering team and in-house tool shop, we manage the design and development of our products end to end. We put prototypes through comprehensive test processes based on maximum operating limits, in line with technical criteria defined in-house. Once the test results confirm performance, durability and reliability, we move on to series production.

By continuing our R&D investment without interruption, we keep developing our technological capabilities, improving product performance and creating innovative engineering solutions.
